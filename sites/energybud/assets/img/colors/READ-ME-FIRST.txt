COLOUR PHOTOS GO HERE
=====================

Two files control this folder:

  available-128.txt   which colours the 1 gallon page offers
  available-74.txt    which colours the half gallon page offers

Photos are named <colour>-<size>.jpg, all lowercase:

  1 gallon (128 oz)          half gallon (74 oz)
  ------------------------   ------------------------
  lime-128.jpg               lime-74.jpg      DONE
  blue-128.jpg               blue-74.jpg      DONE
  pink-128.jpg               pink-74.jpg
  violet-128.jpg             violet-74.jpg
  turquoise-128.jpg          turquoise-74.jpg
  graphite-128.jpg           graphite-74.jpg

You do NOT need all of them. Each page shows only the colours listed in its own
available file, so you can add two now and the rest later. An empty list hides
the picker on that page and it reads exactly as it did before.

Adding a colour
---------------
1. Put the photo in this folder with the right name.
2. Open the matching available-*.txt and delete the "# " in front of that colour.

     # pink      <- hidden
     pink        <- showing

What the photo should look like
-------------------------------
The two that are already here (lime-74.jpg, blue-74.jpg) are the standard: one
bottle, straight on, whole bottle in frame, white background, centred on a
square canvas. Match those and switching colours will look seamless.

The easiest way to get more: the brand's own main product images. Each colourway
has a 01_main_B.jpg shot with that colour's bottle standing alone on white.
Send those and they crop straight into this format.

If you change styles.css or app.js
----------------------------------
Bump the ?v= number in the <link> and <script> tags on every page, or browsers
keep serving the cached copy.
