EnergyBud started with a simple frustration: everyone knows they should drink more water, and almost nobody does. So we built a bottle that keeps score for you.

Our why

A goal you can see is a goal you hit

Willpower fades by mid-afternoon. A visual cue doesn't. By printing the day right onto the bottle — an hourly ladder from your first sip to your last — we turned a vague intention into a scoreboard you glance at fifty times a day.

No app to open, no reminder to snooze. Just a line to beat, every hour, until the gallon's gone.
